description: Governs the simplified selectable volume-percentage engine alongside Biological Media mass balance.

The AAT Core Foundation engine is a designer-selectable alternative to Mass Balance. Size media from linked net exhibit volume: cold below 18°C = 4.0%; otherwise freshwater = 3.5%; marine at or below 100 m³ = 3.0%; marine over 100 m³ = 2.25%. Temperature is never edited in Biological Media: the designer selects the linked exhibit range's lowest point or midpoint average.

**Why:** The simplified AAT foundation is intentionally independent of TAN mass-balance profiles. The cold rule must take precedence so low-temperature designs are not undersized by water-type or size rules.

**How to apply:** Preserve Mass Balance for existing cases by default. Derive AAT temperature from the exhibit range, and freeze the selected temperature basis, engine, version, linked volume, temperature, percentage, rule, and result.
