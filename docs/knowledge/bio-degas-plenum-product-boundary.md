---
name: Bio/Degas plenum product boundary
description: Ownership and promotion rules for project-designed Bio/Degas plenum tanks.
---

A saved Bio/Degas plenum is a project-owned equipment item and contributes one generated line to the linked Design Book Equipment Breakdown. Resaving updates that source while preserving commercial overrides.

**Why:** The engineered plenum must be counted in the project without silently creating reusable library data or duplicating equipment lines.

**How to apply:** Keep one linked project item per saved layout. Treat PIM promotion as a separate, explicit action that creates a reusable FRP tank-family product from an immutable source snapshot; never mutate the source layout or promote automatically.
