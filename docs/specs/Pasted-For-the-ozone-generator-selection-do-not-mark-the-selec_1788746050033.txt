For the ozone generator selection, do not mark the selection as invalid when the next available unit exceeds the required ozone capacity by more than 30%.

Instead, the system should select the most appropriate available ozone generator configuration and display a note showing how much the selected capacity exceeds the required capacity.

The selection logic should consider both the generator model and the quantity of generators required.

For each available ozone generator model:

1. Read the ozone production capacity of the unit from the PIM product model.

Example:

OZ 07 = 7 g/hr
OZ 11 = 11 g/hr
OZ 20 = 20 g/hr

2. Calculate the total installed ozone capacity:

Total Installed Capacity = Unit Capacity × Quantity

Example:

2 × OZ 11 = 22 g/hr

3. The total installed capacity must be equal to or greater than the calculated ozone requirement.

4. The system should evaluate different combinations of unit size and quantity to find the closest available capacity above the required ozone demand.

5. Calculate the percentage above the required capacity:

Oversizing % = ((Installed Capacity ÷ Required Capacity) − 1) × 100

Example:

Required ozone = 18 g/hr

Selected configuration = 2 × OZ 11

Installed capacity = 22 g/hr

Oversizing = ((22 ÷ 18) − 1) × 100 = 22.2%

The displayed selection should therefore show:

Selected Ozone Generator: OZ 11
Quantity: 2
Capacity per Unit: 11 g/hr
Total Installed Capacity: 22 g/hr
Required Capacity: 18 g/hr
Oversizing: 22.2%

If the closest available configuration is more than 30% above the required ozone capacity, the selection should still be allowed.

Do not show an invalid warning.

Instead, display an advisory note such as:

"Closest available ozone generator configuration provides 38% more capacity than the calculated requirement."

The selected equipment allocation, including the PIM product, model, quantity, capacity per unit, total installed capacity, required capacity and oversizing percentage, should then be saved against the system.
