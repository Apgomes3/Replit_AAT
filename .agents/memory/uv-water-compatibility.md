---
name: UV water compatibility
description: Governance rules for material defaults and revision-specific UV water compatibility decisions.
---

UV water compatibility starts from governed material defaults for Saltwater, Seawater, Freshwater, and Brackish water. A UV engineering revision may explicitly override Allowed or Not allowed; using the default is distinct from saving an override.

**Why:** Material guidance should be reused consistently, while a reviewed UV product may need a controlled exception without changing the compatibility of every product made from that material.

**How to apply:** Freeze overrides with the UV engineering revision, copy them into successor drafts, and resolve effective compatibility as revision override first and material default second. Approval requires at least one effectively allowed water type, and Design Book checks must use the same effective value for the system water type.
