---
name: Sand filter bank sizing
description: Recommended quantity behavior for Flow Route sand-filter selections.
---

Sand-filter sizing should recommend one unit for a single-filter duty, then prefer balanced banks of three or four. When more than four units are required, recommend the smallest quantity that is a multiple of three or four. The saved quantity remains an engineer-editable manual override, so quantities such as two are allowed when intentionally selected.

**Why:** A bank must keep one filter available for backwash while the others continue operating; two units do not provide the intended balanced operating logic.

**How to apply:** Keep this rule in sizing guidance and apply actions, not as a hard validation that blocks deliberate engineering overrides.
