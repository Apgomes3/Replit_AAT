---
name: Generated PIM TDS revisions
description: Governance rules for generated Technical Data Sheets sourced from Product Master.
---

Generated PIM Technical Data Sheets keep one stable document identity and product link, while every revision carries its own frozen PIM source snapshot. Editable document content may diverge during review without writing back to Product Master.

**Why:** Product Master remains authoritative, but reviewed or released document evidence must remain reproducible and cannot change when product data is updated or a new draft is generated.

**How to apply:** Regenerate an editable revision in place; after a frozen revision, create the next controlled revision. Never detach or reclassify the generated document, mutate frozen provenance, or invent missing PIM values.
