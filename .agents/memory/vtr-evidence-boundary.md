---
name: VTR evidence boundary
description: Separation between Biological Media source evidence, candidate recommendations, and approved calculator profiles.
---

Published VTR observations, loading benchmarks, upper-performance results, and candidate calculator recommendations remain in a review-only evidence library. They must not become selectable sizing inputs merely because they contain a scalar rate or a familiar profile code.

**Why:** The supplied material mixes measured maxima, applied rates, uncertainty ranges, curve guidance, and provisional recommendations. Flattening those into approved Overall-VTR values would discard tested conditions and could turn a benchmark into a universal design capacity.

**How to apply:** Preserve source wording, conditions, qualifiers, and links between recommendations and source observations. Keep evidence non-promotable and outside profile/calculation queries until a separate controlled engineering review creates an approved profile revision.
